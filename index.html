BEFORE YOU BOOK

Common questions, answered.

Get clear answers about consultation, reports, surgery decisions and what to expect next.

A hernia does not heal on its own. A small hernia that causes no symptoms may only need watching, but one that causes discomfort or keeps growing is usually repaired with surgery. An examination tells you which applies to you.

No. The consultation is about clarity, not persuasion. You'll hear the non-surgical options first, and surgery is discussed only when it is genuinely the right treatment.

Laparoscopic surgery uses small incisions and a camera. Robotic-assisted surgery is a type of keyhole surgery in which the surgeon controls fine robotic instruments from a console. Both usually mean less pain, minimal scarring and a faster recovery than open surgery, and your surgeon will recommend the approach that suits you.

Absolutely. Bring your reports and previous advice, and you'll get an honest, independent assessment with no obligation.

Coverage depends on your policy. Our team helps you with cashless approvals and reimbursement paperwork.

Guidelines followed in India generally consider adults with a BMI above 37.5, or above 32.5 along with weight-related conditions such as type 2 diabetes, who have tried non-surgical weight loss without lasting success. The final decision is made only after a full assessment.

The cost depends on the type of surgery, whether it is laparoscopic or robotic, the hospital, the tests you need and your insurance cover. Robotic surgery can cost more than laparoscopic surgery. You will get a clear estimate after your assessment, before you decide anything.
